I am a UX researcher working on UniProt at EMBL-EBI, the European Bioinformatics Institute. I take a mixed-methods approach — combining quantitative analysis with interviews and observation — to understand how researchers actually use scientific data, and to make those tools easier to work with.

Before EMBL-EBI I was at the User Experience Lab at Seoul National University, working on data-driven behaviour analysis and user modelling. Outside work I drink a lot of coffee and play ultimate, which I have had the privilege of playing for the Korean national team at Asia-Oceania and world events.
